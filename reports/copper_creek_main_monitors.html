Context & Goals

Copper Creek's current EV main speakers have been flagged for replacement following feedback from a sound professional. The band plays everything from small bars and clubs to outdoor festivals and mid-size halls, so the new mains must scale across all venue sizes. This document compares five powered 15" PA tops across price tiers to support a group decision. Tyler is the purchasing owner; all band members are invited to provide input.

Recommendation

Staff Pick: JBL PRX915 — $1,099/ea · $2,198/pair

The JBL PRX915 is the strongest overall match for Copper Creek's needs. The 12-band parametric EQ and Automatic Feedback Suppression are purpose-built for live bands in venues ranging from intimate clubs to outdoor stages. The JBL Pro Connect app gives real-time control from anywhere in the room. The 7-year warranty — the best in the comparison by a wide margin — means the pair should outlast the band's next several years of gigs without concern.

Runner-up: EV EKX-15P ($1,049) for the band that prioritizes touring-grade wood construction, deepest bass extension, and eventual sub integration via the programmatic crossover.  ·  Best value: Turbosound iQ15 ($889) if SPL and wattage are the priority and budget is tight.
